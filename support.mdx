Before opening a support ticket, work through the basic fixes in your product's setup guide — restart your PC, re-download the loader, and inject again. The majority of issues are resolved by those three steps alone. If you are still stuck after that, the support team is ready to help.

## Get Help

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Discord Support" icon="discord" href="https://discord.gg/enmacheats">
    Join the EnmaCheats Discord and open a support ticket. The team will walk you through a fix as quickly as possible.
  </Card>
  <Card title="System Status" icon="signal" href="https://enmacheats.com/status">
    Check whether a product is currently live, mid-update, or down before spending time troubleshooting on your end.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## When Opening a Ticket

To get help as fast as possible, include the following information in your ticket:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your Product">
    State the exact product and build you purchased — for example, "Disconnect Rust Internal" or "Disconnect Wardogs External". This lets support route your ticket to the right person immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What You Tried">
    List which setup steps you completed and the exact point where things went wrong. The more specific you are, the less back-and-forth is needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Exact Error">
    Include the full error message or code, and attach a screenshot if you can. Redact your license key and any other private information before sharing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Check the [status page](https://enmacheats.com/status) before opening a ticket. If a product is currently mid-update, setup attempts will fail until it comes back online — waiting is faster than troubleshooting in that case.
</Note>
