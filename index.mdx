## Quick Setup Overview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare Windows">
    Disable Windows Defender using DControl and install the required DirectX and Visual C++ runtimes. This is required for every product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the Loader">
    Get the loader for your specific product, run it as administrator, and activate your license key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inject & Play">
    Follow your product's injection steps, launch the game, and press `Insert` to open the cheat menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Need Help?">
    If anything goes wrong, check the troubleshooting section in your product guide or [open a ticket on Discord](https://discord.gg/enmacheats).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Always check the [status page](https://enmacheats.com/status) before setting up a new product. If a cheat is mid-update, setup may fail until it's back online.
</Note>
